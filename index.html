Use this when a Bengal sets or ties a mark. Choose "Breaks the record" to move the current holders into that record's history, or "Ties" to add a new line next to them.

Season and career lists re-rank automatically by the mark. Enter the season (2026-27) or career years (2022-26) in the date field. Delete the line that falls off the bottom if the list should stay the same length.
